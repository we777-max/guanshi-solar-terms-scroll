# 四季环境长卷 v2：八景扩展记录

日期：2026-11-12

## 目标与结果

用户反馈第一版每季仅一组院落，场景过短且变化不足。本版沿用已确认的细线淡彩、暖纸画风，将环境从四景扩展到八景，每季两景，增加地形、建筑类型和游览路线。

四季沿长卷依次展开。当前仍为环境底景，人物、动物、书籍、文物及相关交互留待后续制作。所有新画作通过内置 imagegen 生成，原文件保留，项目资产另存。

八幅画每幅2172×724像素。网页采用6%画宽叠合，总布局约为16464×724原图单位，长度约为上一版的1.98倍。此为网页排列长度，未导出单张超宽位图；接缝使用渐变叠合，仍需局部补绘才能精确连续。

Match their exact miniature fine ink-and-watercolor style, warm ivory rice-paper texture, near-frontal handscroll perspective, doors/roofs scale and foreground shoreline. This segment must add a genuinely different landscape and much richer spatial journey, not another composition of two houses facing the river.
Canvas: ONE continuous 3:1 full-bleed original painting around 2172x724 pixels. Water across entire bottom 20-22%, shoreline near y565 of724, continuous walkable riverbank path near y510. Low hills and distant hamlets around upper quarter, pale paper sky. Leftmost edge follows soft bank and young willow from first reference's right edge. Rightmost edge leads through subdued riverside willow/low bank into second reference's left edge.
Composition: a broad winding CREEK runs from distant hills down toward the front river near center. A long elegant modest timber FOOTBRIDGE with fine railings crosses this creek diagonally through the middle-ground, allowing a connecting footpath. Adjacent at left-center a traditional low timber WATERMILL building with a large clearly drawn fixed wooden waterwheel at the creek edge, stone channel and modest cascading water. Watermill is architecture; no machines invented beyond simple wheel/channel. From the middle into the distance a winding uphill lane passes a cluster of SIX TO EIGHT small roofed farm buildings of varied heights and arrangements, bamboo fences, stone retaining walls and tiny steps. These are a continuous village cluster, not copies of reference's two big courtyard houses. Foreground right has an open grassy landing and low orchard trees with delicate new leaves, some branches still bare; background left terraces/ploughed plots and scattered pale spring flowering trees. Several clear empty walkable areas near bridge, mill and lane for later characters. Strong visual focus on bridge and watermill, while the winding hill village gives real narrative depth. All fixed architecture and landscape belong to same traditional Jiangnan-inspired countryside.
Color season: mid-spring, small restrained pale blossoms and a little more muted moss green than earliest-spring reference; warm grey-brown roof tiles, ochre wood, diluted celadon water.
